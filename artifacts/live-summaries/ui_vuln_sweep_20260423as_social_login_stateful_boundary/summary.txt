current_implication:
- `social_login.cgi` is not simply a dead helper on the repaired portal lane
- there is live evidence of a stateful or timing-sensitive path that accepts an arbitrary `fb_user` and issues a guest `authtok` without any observed Facebook token or OAuth verifier reaching the backend
- current evidence is strong enough to reject the earlier "always dead / 500 only" conclusion
- current evidence is not yet clean enough to package as a fully reproducible standalone auth-bypass finding until the exact portal-state precondition is isolated on a fresh lane
