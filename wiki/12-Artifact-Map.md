## Exclusions

Not included:

- firmware binaries
- extracted rootfs
- `runroot`
- `.lab-state`
- large traces and logs
- packet captures
- transient PID/process state
- raw local workspace paths
- private PSIRT correspondence
- superseded report drafts that overstate current impact
